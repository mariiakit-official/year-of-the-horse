In 2026, I chose a new direction through i.c.stars. I’m learning to lead, to build, and to trust myself—even while I’m still learning. The years ahead are hopes, not predictions: a future I want to work toward, one choice at a time.

My guiding idea: success matters, but being a good human being matters more.
